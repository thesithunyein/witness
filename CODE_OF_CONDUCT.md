# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Witness to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- **Be specific.** "This reverts when `expiry` is 0" is a contribution. "This is
  broken" is not.
- **Attack the claim, not the person.** This project publishes a
  [threat model](THREAT-MODEL.md) precisely so its assumptions can be argued with.
  Finding a hole in it is a gift, and the fastest way to be taken seriously here is
  to break something and show the trace.
- **Assume competence.** Contributors may be writing their second language, their
  first smart contract, or their thousandth review.
- **Respect the scope.** Witness is a reference implementation. "It is not a
  production system" is a design decision, not an oversight to be fixed by adding
  features.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, sexualised attention, deliberate
intimidation, publishing others' private information, sustained disruption of
discussions, and dismissing a report of any of the above.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, code comments — and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report anything that concerns you by opening a private security advisory
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) or by contacting the maintainer
([@thesithunyein](https://github.com/thesithunyein)) directly. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer will acknowledge receipt, explain what happens next,
and may remove comments, commits or contributions that violate this document.

## Enforcement

Consequences escalate with severity and repetition: a private warning, then a
temporary ban from project spaces, then a permanent ban. Decisions are recorded in
the issue thread where the report was raised, minus any private detail.
